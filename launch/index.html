2Create your vault

The vault is deployed before the token because its address has to be written into the launch itself. Its address is derived from yours, so nobody can take the one you are about to use.

3Launch on long.xyz

This is long.xyz's own launcher, called directly by your wallet. FLOWETF never touches your supply or your liquidity — the only thing it changes is which address the fee stream is recorded against.

4Register

Registration reads your pool's beneficiary list from the chain and refuses unless the vault is genuinely on it. It is a check, not a claim — which is why a listed token cannot be lying about where its fees go.

FLOWETF distributes trading fees generated by long.xyz pools. Not an investment product, not a fund, not affiliated with Robinhood, long.xyz, or any issuer of the underlying assets. Payout size depends entirely on trading activity and can be zero. You are signing transactions with your own wallet; review each one before approving.
