How it works

Three things happen, every six hours.

01

Fees are collected

Both legs come out of long.xyz at once — your token, and the asset it is anchored to.

02

Half your token is burned

Sent to 0x…dEaD, gone for good. The other half pays the gas. This runs every cycle.

03

Holders get the stock

All of it, split by how much they hold. Pushed to their wallets — nobody claims, nobody signs.

Questions

Straight answers

Do holders have to claim?

Never. There is no claim button anywhere, on purpose — fake claim pages are how most wallets get drained. The stock is pushed to holders. If a site asks you to connect and claim a FLOWETF payout, it is not us.

Who receives the payout?

Anyone holding at least $100 of the token at the snapshot, valued live against marketcap. Everyone above that line shares the payout in proportion to what they hold. What you receive is whatever the token is anchored to — usually a tokenized stock, though a pool can be paired against any asset. Each token's card states exactly what it pays.

What if there are barely any fees that cycle?

Distributions wait until the pot is worth $500, because gas is charged per wallet and shredding a tiny pot across hundreds of holders wastes most of it. Nothing is lost while it waits, and after 7 days it is paid out regardless of size.

What does it cost me?

5% of what actually gets distributed. No setup fee, no monthly fee, and nothing at all on a cycle that pays out nothing.

Can you run off with the stock?

The contract can only send the one stock token it was deployed with, only to addresses that genuinely hold your token, and at most 70% of the balance per cycle. It has no withdraw-to-owner path on the distribution side. Not audited by an outside firm — 35 behavioural tests against a local EVM, and short enough to read end to end.
